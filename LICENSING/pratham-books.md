# Partnership email — Pratham Books (StoryWeaver)

> DRAFT. StoryWeaver stories are published under open licences (mostly CC BY 4.0), so no permission is needed to display them with credit. This note is a courtesy and a way to agree on good practice (and to ask about bulk access and audio).

**To:** [StoryWeaver / Pratham Books team]
**Subject:** Comic Gali: a read-and-listen reader for StoryWeaver stories

Dear [Name],

Comic Gali is a new site for reading and listening to Indian comics and illustrated stories in ten languages. We would like to include a shelf of StoryWeaver stories under their CC licences, always with the author, illustrator, translator, licence and a link back to the story page.

We would like to ask:
1. Is there a preferred way to access the stories in bulk (API or export), and any attribution wording you would like us to use?
2. Are you interested in recorded narration instead of browser text-to-speech? We could credit the narrators and share the audio back under the same licence.
3. Anything you would like us to avoid.

We will not remove or alter credits, and we respond to takedown requests promptly.

With thanks,
[Your name], Comic Gali
