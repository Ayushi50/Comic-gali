**What “public domain” means here.** The tales themselves are centuries old. The wording, translations and art in this project are new works made for Comic Gali and offered under CC BY 4.0. We did not copy text or images from any modern retelling, comic or illustrated edition.

**Translations** into Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi were written by Claude (an AI) and have not been reviewed by native speakers. They must be reviewed before launch (see MORNING_REPORT.md).
